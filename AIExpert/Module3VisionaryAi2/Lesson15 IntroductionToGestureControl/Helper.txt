👉 Gesture control = Using hand/body movements to give commands

🧠 Examples
✋ Moving your hand to control a game
👆 Swiping in the air to change slides
🤚 Showing a palm to pause a video
👍 Thumbs up to like or confirm

📱 Real-world uses
Smartphones (air gestures)
Gaming systems (like motion sensors)
Smart TVs
Virtual Reality (VR)

Computer Vision projects (like your OpenCV work 👀)

Gesture control usually means:
Camera captures your hand
Program detects gesture
Performs action

Example:
✋ Open hand → Start
✊ Closed fist → Stop
👉 Pointing → Select

🎯 What is a framework?
A framework is a pre-built structure (set of tools, libraries, and rules) that helps you develop applications faster and more easily.

🧠 Simple definition
👉 Framework = Ready-made foundation for building software

Eg: React, MediaPipe, Tensorflow
