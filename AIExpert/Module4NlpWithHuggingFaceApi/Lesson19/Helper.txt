NLP with Hugging face

🌟 NLP – Natural Language Processing
NLP is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that teaches computers to understand, read, and write human language.
Think of it like this:
Computers normally “speak” in 0s and 1s.
NLP helps computers understand words, sentences, and meanings.
Example:
You type a question: "What is the weather today?"
Computer understands your words and answers: "It’s sunny."

🔹 Real-life examples of NLP
Chatbots – like the assistant on a website
Google Translate – translates languages
Voice assistants – Alexa, Siri
Spell check – in Word or Google Docs
Text summarization – making a long article short

🌟 Hugging Face
Hugging Face is a website and tool that gives ready-made AI models for NLP.
You don’t have to write all the AI from scratch.
It’s like a library of smart brains that can:
Understand text
Translate languages
Answer questions
Summarize stories

🌈 Simple analogy
NLP = teaching a robot to read and understand human language
Hugging Face = robot toolkit with lots of pre-trained brains
